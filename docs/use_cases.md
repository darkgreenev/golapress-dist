# Use Cases

goLaPress fits best when you want a WordPress-like publishing workflow without a PHP stack.

Good fits:

## Blogs And Publishing Sites

- editorial websites
- company blogs
- news or announcement sites
- personal publishing sites

Why it fits:

- posts, pages, media, and revisions are first-class
- the admin UI is designed for routine content work
- backups and restore flows are built in

## Agency And Client Sites

- small business sites
- client brochure sites
- managed-hosting deployments
- agency-maintained publishing stacks

Why it fits:

- single-binary deployment is operationally simple
- plugins and themes are bounded instead of open-ended
- the admin surface is predictable for non-developers

## Documentation And Knowledge Bases

- product docs
- internal wikis
- structured editorial content
- support or help-center sites

Why it fits:

- pages and navigation are core features
- theme content and plugins can shape the presentation safely
- the content model is simple enough to keep documentation organized

## Internal Portals

- team portals
- intranet pages
- operations dashboards
- content hubs with controlled access

Why it fits:

- admin and operator tools are built in
- roles and auth are part of the platform
- the stack stays small enough to manage on a VPS

## Light Commerce Sites

- catalog-driven storefronts
- product-focused sites
- simple commerce workflows

Why it fits:

- commerce is plugin-based and bounded
- the core CMS can stay focused on content and site operations
- plugin screens and CLI support can be added in a controlled way
